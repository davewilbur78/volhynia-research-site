About this project

This site exists because the Ukrainian archives holding these records have only recently become accessible — the result of a legal effort to open records that had been effectively sealed — and because the raw scans are in Russian and Hebrew, unindexed, and scattered across archives whose own holdings don't always match what history would predict. This project pulls that material into one place, in English, with a scan behind every citation.

Built and maintained by David Wilbur for the Belohorodka Research Group. Corrections and leads welcome.
